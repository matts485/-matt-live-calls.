Frequently Asked Questions

How do I request a call?

Go to the Book page and submit the entertainment call request form.

Are the calls live?

Yes. The experience is arranged as a live conversation with a performer.

Is my booking automatically confirmed?

No. Requests are reviewed first. Availability and confirmation details are provided before the call.

How long is a call?

Call length depends on the experience and availability. Details can be discussed during the booking process.

How can I ask a question before booking?

Visit the Contact page to send an email with your question.

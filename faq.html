Frequently Asked Questions

How do I request a call?

Use the Book page and submit the booking form with your preferred details.

Are the calls live?

Yes. Confirmed bookings are live entertainment conversations with a performer.

Is my booking automatically confirmed?

No. Booking requests are reviewed first. A call is confirmed only after availability and details have been reviewed.

How long is a call?

Call duration depends on the booking arrangement and will be provided when your booking is confirmed.

How can I ask a question before booking?

Visit the Contact page to send an email.

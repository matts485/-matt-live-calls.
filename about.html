About the Experience

The Private Fan Experience is designed for fans who want a more personal and interactive entertainment experience.

Each booking is arranged as a live conversation with a performer. Calls can include friendly conversation, interactive moments, storytelling, and other entertainment.

Every booking request is reviewed before a call is confirmed. Availability and call details are provided during the booking process.
